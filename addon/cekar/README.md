# Cekar

Shared shopping lists and shared expenses for one household, in the Home Assistant sidebar. "Done shopping" turns the checked items and the receipt total into expenses. Everyone who can open Home Assistant can use the lists; the household chooses who takes part in shared expenses. Opens through Ingress and follows your Home Assistant theme.

Requires Home Assistant Supervisor 2026.10.3 or later.

Documentation: https://domengorjup.github.io/cekar/
