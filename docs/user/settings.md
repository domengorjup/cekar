# Settings

![Settings](../assets/screenshots/settings-desktop-light.png)

## Shared expenses

Everyone who has opened Cekar is listed. Switch a person on to make them a member of shared expenses. Members appear in splits, "for" choices and balances. Everyone can use the shopping lists, member or not.

Turning someone off asks for confirmation. Their history is kept, and a balance they still have stays visible until it is settled.

**Add person** (standalone mode, or when Cekar cannot see Home Assistant users) adds someone by name. They start as a member.

## Lists

Create, rename and delete lists here, and reorder them with the arrows. The order is the order of the chips on the Lists tab.

## Who are you?

Shown only when Cekar does not get your identity from Home Assistant. **Switch person** goes back to the picker.
